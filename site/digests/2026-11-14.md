Expect scrutiny over fraud liability and consent UX as adoption scales.

## Research & Academic Breakthroughs

### AlphaProof Code Verifies Signal's Core Encryption With Three Million Lines of Lean Proofs

DeepMind unveiled AlphaProof Code, which it describes as a breakthrough in verifying complex software at depth; its agents wrote three million lines of proofs in Lean to formally verify the core encryption of Signal. Formal verification of production cryptographic code at this scale is a genuine milestone — historically such efforts have been limited to small, hand-curated codebases because proof engineering was prohibitively labor-intensive. If agent-generated proofs can be made routinely trustworthy, the implications span security-critical infrastructure, regulated industries, and open-source supply-chain assurance. The result also strengthens the case that AI-for-mathematics work is converting into practical software-engineering value, not just benchmark wins.

Source: [DeepMind Blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/introducing-alphaproof-code/)

### AlphaGenome 2 Advances Prediction of How Cells Read DNA and How Variants Drive Disease

DeepMind released AlphaGenome 2, focused on predicting how DNA sequences and their variations impact specific human cell types and disease. The model's cell-type-specific framing is the key advance: most genomic models predict broadly, but therapeutic and diagnostic value depends on resolving effects at the resolution of individual tissues and cell states. Better variant-effect prediction is foundational for target discovery, rare-disease diagnosis, and functional annotation of the vast non-coding genome. With this release, DeepMind continues to build a credible franchise in computational biology that operates independently of its LLM business — and one with a plausible multi-year path toward clinical relevance.

Source: [DeepMind Blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/alphagenome-2-deciphering-how-cells-read-dna-to-better-understand-disease/)
