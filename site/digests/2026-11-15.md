## Research & Academic Breakthroughs

### DeepMind's AlphaProof Code Verifies Signal's Core Encryption with Three Million Lines of Lean Proofs

DeepMind introduced AlphaProof Code, which it describes as a breakthrough in verifying complex software at depth—agents wrote three million lines of Lean proofs to formally verify Signal's core encryption. This is a landmark result for formal verification: applying AI-generated proof engineering to real-world, security-critical cryptographic code at a scale no human team could practically achieve. The significance extends beyond Signal—formal verification has historically been too labor-intensive for mainstream software, and AI-driven proof generation could change that calculus. If the approach generalizes, it opens a path to verified-critical-infrastructure software across finance, healthcare, and defense. It also strengthens DeepMind's position in AI-for-mathematics and program synthesis, a domain with few credible competitors.
