### BuggyMLE Benchmark Scales Machine Learning Engineering Difficulty

Researchers have introduced BuggyMLE, a framework addressing the saturation of existing MLE benchmarks by injecting controlled bugs into working ML repositories, generating over 6,600 bug-injected variants and nearly 260,000 lines of injected code across 222 source repositories. The work introduces the Code Complexity Index (CCI), a difficulty metric based on ten structural features that predicts average solve rates across eight public coding benchmarks with R² = 0.951. On BuggyMLE-Bench-Lite, the top solve rate drops from 95% to 9% as difficulty scales from easiest to hardest tier across 14 evaluated models, demonstrating substantial headroom for improvement. Fine-tuning a Qwen3.5-9B model on a synthesized training curriculum enabled it to rival frontier models on the new benchmark, with transfer effects raising SWE-bench Lite resolve rates from 24% to 33% and LiveCodeBench v6 pass@1 from 69% to 71%. This work provides the research community with difficulty-graded evaluation infrastructure at a moment when repository-level benchmarks have rapidly saturated, potentially guiding more efficient agent training.
